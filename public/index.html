⚡ About the Creator

Danyal Khan is a self-taught Ethical Hacker, Bug Bounty Hunter & Web Developer from Dir Upper, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KPK), Pakistan.

Coming from a remote area with limited resources, Danyal proved that passion matters more than a degree. Without any formal training or institute, he taught himself network security, ethical hacking and full-stack web development from scratch.

At just 17 years old, he built DANI SCAN - one of the first 100% free, open-access web-based network diagnostics platforms from Dir Upper. It gives instant Ping, Port & DNS checks with No Login, No Registration and No Payment.

His mission is simple - to make cybersecurity tools free and easy for students, developers and security researchers, specially for the youth of Pakistan who want to learn hacking the right, legal way.

🎯 Vision: To keep essential cybersecurity tools 100% free and inspire self-taught learners from remote areas of KPK to build innovative tech solutions.
